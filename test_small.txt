Apple Inc. reported revenue of $119.6 billion for Q1 2024, representing a 5% increase from the previous year. The company's gross margin was 38.2%, slightly down from 38.3% in Q1 2023. Operating expenses rose 3% to $14.7 billion. Net income reached $24.2 billion, up 10% year-over-year. The Services segment grew 14% to $23.2 billion, while iPhone sales increased 2% to $69.1 billion. Apple continues to face challenges in the Chinese market and supply chain constraints.
